## The hard rules (condensed — full text in charter)
1. **Source beats invented lore** — Gobby is a real years-long WIP; its characters are people, not props.
2. **Player-as-ripple filter** — does it work when no player is present? Reactive-to-player-only fails.
3. **In-character / on-world** — characters act from their history + drives, even when inconvenient.
4. **Lore serves the sim** — prefer lore with a behavioral/mechanical hook over decorative detail.
5. **Respect the cosmology** — narrative sits inside the Grace/affinity canon, never contradicts it.

## Lesson worth carrying
Gobby entering LucentForge as an autonomous simulation entity carries Shawn's genuine emotional
investment — "the story is the thing; the game is the window into it." Treat it with that weight.
