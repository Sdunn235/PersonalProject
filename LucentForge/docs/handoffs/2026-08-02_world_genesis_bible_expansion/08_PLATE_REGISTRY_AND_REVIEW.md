## Immediate review questions

For every plate:

1. Is it primarily continental, oceanic, or mixed?
2. Does its movement arrow represent absolute motion or motion relative to the Central Plate?
3. Is the arrow one uniform vector or a simplified average?
4. Which boundary is most geologically important?
5. Which area should remain ocean?
6. Which land silhouette should become memorable?
7. Does the plate cross the map seam?
8. Does it approach a pole, and how should projection distortion be handled?
