# LucentForge Climate, Hydrology & Biome Addendum v1

**Status:** Early draft / dependency document — derivation rules only; **nothing is canonized** (see §C8)
**Section IDs:** §C1–§C8

## Purpose

This addendum defines the **order and rules** by which climate, hydrology, and biomes are derived from the planetary structure established in `lucentforge_world_foundation_v1.md` and `lucentforge_planetary_tectonics_addendum_v1.md`. It canonizes no specific winds, currents, rivers, or biome placements. Cite it when climate/biome data is derived or generated.

---

## §C1 — Dependency Rule

Climate and biome maps must be derived **after**:

- projection and latitude;
- elevation;
- ocean basins;
- mountain systems;
- broad atmospheric assumptions.

**Do not paint horizontal biome stripes directly from latitude.**

## §C2 — Climate Inputs

At minimum: latitude; elevation; distance to ocean; prevailing wind; mountain barriers; ocean-current temperature; seasonality; fantasy/affinity modifications.

## §C3 — Rain Shadows

Mountains create wet windward slopes and potentially dry leeward interiors. This relationship should influence forests, grasslands, deserts, glaciers, river sources, and settlement potential.

## §C4 — Hydrology

Major rivers should:

- begin in wet or glaciated highlands;
- follow descending terrain;
- merge into larger systems;
- terminate in oceans, lakes, wetlands, or interior basins;
- split mainly in deltas or exceptional terrain.

Watersheds should be represented **separately** from visible river lines.

## §C5 — Closing Ocean Consequences

The Deep Ocean system (§W6, §T5) may create: trenches; volcanic arcs; restricted currents; enclosed or semi-enclosed seas; high coastal seismic risk; unusual nutrient circulation; isolated marine ecosystems.

## §C6 — Biomes

Biomes are **outcomes, not first causes.** The canonical map should support transitional zones rather than sharp arbitrary boundaries.

## §C7 — Affinity Interaction

Affinity may affect comfort, chemistry, ecology, material behavior, and local climate, but it must not become an excuse to ignore physical causal chains. Distinguish:

- baseline physical climate;
- affinity-modified climate;
- cultural interpretations;
- gameplay approximations.

This is consistent with the biochem/affinity doctrine in `lucentforge_biochem_affinity_addendum_v1.md` and the Grace affinity foundation — affinity modulates a physical substrate; it does not replace it.

## §C8 — Deferred Work

No specific global wind cells, ocean currents, rivers, or biome placements are canonized here.
